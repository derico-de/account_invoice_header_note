Account Invoice Header Note

A rich text note above the invoice lines.

Why this module?

Odoo prints the Terms and Conditions below the invoice lines. There is no counterpart above them. Anything that belongs at the top of an invoice — a greeting, a delivery reference, a project or contract number, a short explanation of what is being billed — has to be squeezed into the first invoice line or into the address block.

This module adds a Header Note field to the invoice. It behaves exactly like the Terms and Conditions, only at the other end of the lines table: rich text, typed per invoice, shown above the lines in the backend and printed above the lines table on the PDF.

Type it where it prints

The editor sits on the Invoice Lines tab, directly above the lines. Bold, italic, lists and links are available, because it is the same rich text widget Odoo uses for the Terms and Conditions.

  • Works on customer invoices, credit notes, vendor bills and receipts.
  • Copied along when an invoice is duplicated.
  • Stays editable after posting, just like the Terms and Conditions.
  • An empty note prints nothing at all — no stray paragraph, no extra spacing.

Nothing to configure

Install it and the field is there. There is no company-wide default and no template list, by design: this is free text that differs from invoice to invoice.

If you want reusable, conditional text blocks chosen from a list instead, the OCA modules base_comment_template and account_comment_template cover that case, and can be installed alongside this one.

Technical notes

  • Adds the field header_note (fields.Html) to account.move.
  • Two view inheritances, no core view is replaced: the form anchors before invoice_line_ids, the report before the invoice lines table.
  • German translation included.
  • Depends on account only.
